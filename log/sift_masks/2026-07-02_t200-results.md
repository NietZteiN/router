### Target Date: 2026-07-02 (T=200 science: mask recovers utility at scale; exact unlearn preserves it)
- **Hypotheses / what we're testing:** The full T=200 SIFT-Masks run on Llama-3.2-1B, scored on the
  repo's OU metrics. **H1 (scale collapse):** plain FT+Merge (`merge_full`, no mask) collapses at
  T=200 — predict `model_utility` near base (~0.42). CONFIRM if merge_full mu ≪ sift_full; REFUTE if
  merge_full mu ≈ sift_full. **H2 (mask recovers utility):** the per-task mask (`sift_full`) recovers
  per-task utility — predict mu near the joint-FT/k=1 upper bound (~0.74). **H3 (exact unlearning):**

  level) with mu roughly unchanged. **H4 (mask is the active ingredient):** `merge_unlearn` forgets
  as well as `sift_unlearn` but stays collapsed on utility.
