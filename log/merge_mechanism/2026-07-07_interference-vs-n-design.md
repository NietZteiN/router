### Target Date: 2026-07-07 (interference vs N — design + pre-registration)
- **Hypotheses / what we're testing:** How does utility degrade as N per-author LoRAs are merged, N ∈ {1,2,4,8,16,32,64,128,200}? Prior k-scaling (`../../tofu_sisa_lora/reports/SCALE_REPORT_2026-06-12.md`) varied shard *count* with total data fixed (200/k authors per shard); no experiment has fixed one-LoRA-per-author granularity and varied the number merged. Pre-registered:
  - **H1 (interference, not just dilution):** each probe author's own-author recall (forget_rouge via `--eval_shard_id`) decays **monotonically** with N under a true-scale mean (`additive_mean`, weight 1/N). CONFIRM: mean probe forget_rouge strictly decreasing across the ladder (allowing smoke noise ±0.02 between adjacent N). REFUTE: recall flat once dilution is accounted for, or non-monotone beyond noise.
  - **H2 (coverage vs damage):** headline `model_utility` vs N is **non-monotone** — rises first (more retain-set authors covered by the merge) then falls/plateaus toward base (~0.42) as interference dominates. CONFIRM: mu(N) has an interior maximum. REFUTE: monotone rise to a plateau ≥ the N≈8–32 values (interference never dominates coverage), or monotone fall from N=1.
  - **H3 (geometry→behavior, the open Exp-1 question):** a probe adapter's mean col(B) principal-angle overlap with its co-merged set predicts its recall drop — Spearman ρ > 0 pooled over (probe × N). CONFIRM: ρ ≥ 0.4, p < 0.05. REFUTE: ρ ≈ 0 or negative.
