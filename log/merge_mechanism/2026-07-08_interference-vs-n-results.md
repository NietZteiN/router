- **What worked / hypothesis verdict:**
  - **H1 SUPPORTED with a refinement (saturating, not gradual):** recall decays monotonically only up to N≈8, then floors — by **N=8 the probes have already lost ~85% of their extractable signal** (drop 0.073 of 0.086) and sit at the base-model floor from N=8 to N=200 (rouge 0.41–0.42 ≈ floor 0.404). ppl is strictly monotone but decelerating (8.0→8.5 over N=32→200). Fact entanglement under a true mean is a *fast early collapse*, not gradual erosion across the ladder.
  - **H2 REFUTED:** mu(N) has no interior maximum — it is flat at 0.459 ± 0.002 for every N ≥ 1 (fig1/fig3). No coverage gain (retain_prob flat at 0.234–0.236: at weight 1/N no author is recalled strongly enough to lift the retain rows) and no late damage (real/world components flat). The 1/N mean of per-author adapters is a constant "TOFU-style adapter" worth +0.033 mu over base, independent of N. It also strictly dominates the √r-convention dare_ties at N=200 (0.4597 vs 0.4198 ≈ base).
  - **H3 REFUTED in sign (relationship real, direction opposite):** higher col(B) alignment with the co-merged set predicts a **smaller** drop (ρ = −0.675). Interpretation: alignment with the shared output subspace means your write-directions *survive the averaging* (they are reinforced by the crowd), while idiosyncratic directions are scaled away by 1/N. Geometry does predict merge damage — but overlap is *protection in the mean*, not interference. Caveats: pooled across N (within-N has only 5 probes); single seed.
- **Observations:** (i) The prior k-scaling "dilution curve" (mu 0.74→0.42) conflated shard size with count: at fixed per-author granularity the utility axis carries **no N-dependence at all** — everything interesting happens in per-author recall, which mu cannot see (fig1 vs fig2). (ii) The N=200 additive_mean point beats the dare_ties convention by +0.04 mu, consistent with the rsLoRA √r-inflation story. (iii) `forget_truth_ratio`/`forget_quality` are NaN for single-author probes (TOFU perturbed splits don't cover every author's questions) — recall read from forget_rouge/forget_ppl as in Exp-3; harmless to mu. (iv) Silent-failure checks clean: no NaN mu, retain_ppl 7.7–7.9 everywhere, real/world bit-identical across probe jobs. (v) The isolated per-author adapter itself is a weak fact-server (iso rouge 0.49 vs joint-ft 0.91 on the same author) — per-author isolation costs recall even before any merging.
